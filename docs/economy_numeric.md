### Bot Chip

[https://transformers-forged-to-fight.fandom.com/wiki/Bot_Chip](https://transformers-forged-to-fight.fandom.com/wiki/Bot_Chip)

- as_of_2023: revid 10610, saved 2023-01-11T01:31:00Z

  - November 2022 was the very last month to spend Bot Chips on Motormaster, Windblade, Ultra Magnus, and Barricade. You'd think they could buy Bots galore for the finale events but there were '''''absolutely no provisions. And routine Bot Chip Arenas kept running despite nothing to spend them on!'''''

  - *3-Star - 10,000 + 5,000 per purchase
  - *4-Star - 100,000 + 40,000 per purchase
  - *5-Star - 1,000,000 + 300,000 per purchase
  - * ''Take on the Class Clash Special Team everyday to earn Extra Points and Class ORE-13! You can also purchase Counter-Class Sparks and ORE-13 in the Store with Class Clash Chips, and Class Premium Bot Crystals for Energon!''
  - * ''For the duration of the Event, you can earn Class Clash Chips for every successful Raid you complete. Use these Chips in the Store to purchase Class Spark Essence and ORE-13!''
  - * ''There will also be a 7-Day Raid Ascent event running alongside the Class Clash Event. This event features the regular rewards for Raid Ascent, but also includes a small amount of Class Clash Chips in the rewards!''
  - * ''We’ve updated the Class Crystals to include all of our regularly available Bots! This means that the only ones not available are Thundercracker, and Bumblebee (Generations), while Bumblebee (DOTM) will be available as a 2 and 3 Star, but not as a 4-Star.''
  - * ''On July 3, a free-for-all Arena will be available featuring all the class special teams. The top rewards for this event will be 6x 4-Star Class Bot Crystals (1x OF EACH CLASS)!''
  - *Chips after the event were converted 1:1 gold and then Shards remained for future events.
  - ** ''There will be a Special 7-Day Calendar weekly to give you login rewards of Beast Wars Chips. Login in daily so to not miss out!''
  - Some 2022 "sidequest" [[Special Mission|Spotlight Missions]] had '''Faction Chips''' which can be used to buy faction-specific Bots and crystals. They could be carried over between faction events.

- as_of_2023: revid 10734, saved 2023-01-13T15:57:16Z
  - * Special 3 Unlocked - Most mission bots have their Special 3 locked by default unless they have this mod. Reserved for bosses and all Master-level bots.
  - * '''Attack Boost''' - +25~50~100~200~300~500% Attack (?)
  - *'''Health Boost''' - +25~50~100~200~300~500% Health
  - * '''Prime Boost '''- 25~50~100~150% Attack and Health
  - * '''Armor''' - Gain an Armor Buff, reducing damage from attacks by 15~25~50~75~90%.
  - * '''Armor Timed''' - gains an Armor Buff, reducing attack from opponents by 25~50~75~90~% for 7.5 seconds. Recharges in 15 seconds.
  - * '''Armor Pierce''' - Attack bypass 15~25~50~75~90% of the opponent's current Armor.
  - * '''Armor Pierce Timed''' -  Attacks bypass 25~50~75~90% of the opponent's current Armor for 7.5 seconds. Recharges in 15 seconds.
  - * '''Adaptive''' - Cycles between +39~46~55~65~90% Physical or Energy Resistance every 7.5 seconds.
  - * '''Cycle Resist''' - Cycles through Melee, Ranged, and Special Damage resistances at 50~75~100~125~150~200% damage resistance.
  - * '''Gain Armor''' - When stuck, there's a 6% to gain a 15~<s>25</s>~40~55~60~85% Armor Buff. Additionally, this Buff automatically activates when a Special Attack is activated by the opponent.
  - * '''Energon Shield''' - When Energon shield is active, absorb (tier 1)~8~18~30% of opponent's Attack as Health. When Energon Shield is down, all resistances are reduced by -(tier 1)~35~50~65%.
  - * '''Physical Resistance''' - Resist 15~25~50~75~90% Physical Damage.
  - * '''Energy Resistance''' - Resist 15~25~50~75~90% Energy Damage.
  - * '''Buff Duration''' - Increase the duration of buffs by 25~50~75~100~150~200%.
  - * '''Debuff Duration '''- Increase the duration of debuffs by 25~50~75~100~150~200%.
  - * '''Enhanced Bleed''' - Bleeds inflicted by this Bot last 25~50~75~100~125% longer.
  - * '''Randomizer''' - At the start of the fight and every 18 seconds, activates a random, Attack, Armor, or Resistance Buff. Each buff increases respective attributes by 10-15~20~30%. Buffs stack up to 10 max.
  - * '''Block Proficiency''' - Reduces incoming damage from enemy attacks that are successfully blocked by 7.5~~25~37.5%%
  - * '''Reflect Ranged''' - Reflects incoming ranged attacks while Blocking, dealing 85~100~120~140~170% of their original damage.
  - * '''Shock Block''' - Blocked Melee Attacks cause opponents to take 10~25~50~100~150% of opponent's Attack as Shock Damage over 4 seconds.
  - * '''Critical Damage''' - Increase Critical Damage by 25~50~100~150~200%
  - * '''Critical Rate''' - Increases Critical Rate by +15~25~50~75~90%
  - * '''Critical Damage Timed''' - Increase Critical Damage by +50~100~200~400~600% for 7.5 seconds. Recharges in 15 seconds.
  - * '''Critical Rate Timed'''  - Increase Critical Rate by +50~(tier 3-4)~90% for 7.5 seconds. Recharges in 15 seconds.
  - * '''Target Vitals '''- Landing 5 Critical Hits grants the next Critical Hit an additional ~110~120~140% of Attack as Bonus Damage and Stuns the opponent for 1.5 seconds.''' '''
  - * '''Evade Melee''' - 5~10~15~20% chance to Evade Melee Attacks.
  - * '''Evade Ranged''' - 5~10~15~20% chance to Evade Ranged Attacks.
  - * '''Evade Melee Timed '''- 10~15~20~25~30% chance to evade Melee attacks for 7.5 seconds. Recharges in 15 seconds.
  - * '''Evade Ranged Timed''' - 10~15~20~25~30% chance to evade Ranged attacks for 7.5 seconds. Recharges in 15 seconds.
  - * '''Evade Power Gain''' - When Evading a Melee Attack, gain 26~31~36~43~51~60% of a Power Bar.
  - * '''Stagger''' - Heavy Attacks inflict the opponent with a Stun for 1.5~2~2.5~3~3.5~4 seconds, Power Lock for 4~5~7~9~11~13 seconds, or a 25~35~50~65~85~100% Armor Break for 7 seconds. (Also makes the bot much more likely to use Heavy Attacks)
